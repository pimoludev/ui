<ReleaseSection id="highlights" label="At a glance" title="What matters in beta.5">

- **Navbar gains a real mobile menu.** `NavbarMenu` opens a modal panel from any of four edges, supports partial sizes or full screen, optional native animation and Drawer focus, dismissal and scroll locking.
- **Motion becomes a shared contract.** A reduced-motion policy, a subtree opt-out and the published `@pimolu/web-styles/motion.css` entry keep interaction feedback consistent without animating layout or reading content.
- **Accordion expands at natural height.** Expansion and collapse use CSS grid tracks, handle unequal heights and rapid toggles, and can be disabled with `animated={false}`.
- **Validation feedback stays subtle.** Form errors and surfaces gain quiet transitions while static layouts, typography and existing component-specific motion remain unchanged.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="packages" label="npm beta" title="Published packages">

<ReleaseSection id="components" label="React and CSS" title="Components and interactions">

- NavbarMenu keeps the existing inline composition on wide navbars and switches to a modal Drawer panel inside narrow collapsible containers.
- The panel accepts left, right, top or bottom placement, partial sizes or full screen, an accessible label and close label, custom styles and typed named parts. Desktop composition and focus restoration remain intact.
- Accordion animates expansion and collapse at the content's natural height. Closed content is inert and removed from the accessibility tree immediately; `animated={false}` removes entry, exit and chevron motion.
- FormError and FormField validation messages enter with a short fade and translation. Cards, badges, avatars, table rows, command items, navigation controls and Sidebar sizing use quiet surface transitions.
- Reduced motion always wins. `data-pimolu-motion="off"` disables animation and transition within a subtree, including pseudo-elements and custom children.
- Tabs, SegmentedControl, splitters, chart exploration and existing overlay feedback keep their component-specific behavior.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="limits" label="Preview" title="Known limits">

- This is a beta: component APIs and visual tokens may still change before 0.1.0 stable.
- The seven complex-component assistive-technology reviews remain open; browser tests do not replace manual visual and screen-reader review.
- CSS opt-out suppresses visual movement but does not change existing overlay presence timers. Use `animated={false}` when an immediate close or unmount is required.
- Native, Angular and web registries remain unimplemented.
- The Playground bundle still exceeds the Vite 500 kB warning threshold.

</ReleaseSection>
