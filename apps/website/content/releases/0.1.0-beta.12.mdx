<ReleaseSection id="highlights" label="At a glance" title="What matters in beta.12">

- **TiltedCarousel joins the carousel families.** A rail of separated, slightly rotated cards with `tilt`, `gap`, `slidesPerView`, `slideWidth` and `viewportHeight`. Arrows, dots and the keyboard share the measured page model.
- **Optional endless scroll.** `autoScroll` repeats decorative slides once and wraps by one repetition, so content circulates A to Z to A with no visible end. Hover, focus, reduced motion and a built-in pause button stop it; the loop is disabled when a slide contains interactive content.
- **Carousel and modal surfaces go inactive.** Mirrored and pointer-inert slides are hidden from the keyboard and assistive technology; a dismissed modal becomes inert as its exit animation starts.
- **Sitemap freshness.** Every canonical URL now emits `lastModified`, from the authored date of releases and articles or the latest published release date elsewhere.
- **Showcase.** games.pimolu.com joins the project gallery, ordered newest first.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="rail" label="Added" title="An editorial rail that loops when it should">

- `TiltedCarousel` accepts controlled or uncontrolled selection, localized labels, additive named parts including a playback button, and a CSS entry point at `@pimolu/web-styles/tilted-carousel.css`.
- `slidesPerView` resizes cards so the requested count fits; `slideWidth` overrides the computed width and `viewportHeight` sets the track height.
- `autoScroll`, `autoScrollDirection`, `speed`, `pauseLabel` and `resumeLabel` configure the decorative loop. Pages are hidden while the endless rail runs.
- The playground and website reference demonstrate basic use, controlled selection, localized labels, visible-card sizing, the endless scroll with direction and pause controls, a real services gallery and named-part customization.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="limits" label="Preview" title="Known limits">

- This is a beta: component APIs and visual tokens may still change before 0.1.0 stable.
- Endless scroll is limited to decorative slides; a rail containing links, buttons or other interactive content stays manually navigable.
- Carousel navigation is measured and rendered on the client after mount; the server HTML does not contain arrows or dots.
- The human design review and the assistive-technology review remain open; automated checks are not a screen-reader or WCAG certification.
- Native, Angular and direct CDN adapters remain unimplemented.

</ReleaseSection>
