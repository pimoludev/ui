<ReleaseSection id="highlights" label="At a glance" title="What matters in beta.9">

- **Six carousel families are available.** ThumbnailCarousel, CoverflowCarousel, PeekCarousel, StackCarousel, ArcCarousel and MultiCarousel share one engine and expose named parts. `Carousel` remains the simple autoplay entry point.
- **Modal joins the library.** Modal, ModalTrigger, ModalContent, ModalHeader, ModalTitle, ModalDescription, ModalFooter and ModalClose provide sm/md/lg/xl sizes with managed focus.
- **Non-focusable triggers become keyboard reachable.** Dialog, Drawer, DropdownMenu, Popover, Tooltip, HoverCard, ContextMenu and Popconfirm add `tabIndex={0}` and `role="button"` only when the cloned child is not natively focusable.
- **Documentation is easier to scan.** Carousels have their own category and overview, Class names and CSS variables use readable grids, and API type columns no longer force a horizontal table scroll.
- **No breaking change.** No public component or hook was removed. The new components and optional props are additive.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="carousels" label="New" title="Six carousel families">

- ThumbnailCarousel, CoverflowCarousel, PeekCarousel, StackCarousel, ArcCarousel and MultiCarousel share index state, loop, labels, live announcements and keyboard navigation.
- CarouselContent, CarouselSlide, CarouselPrevious, CarouselNext, CarouselDots, CarouselThumbnails, CarouselThumbnail and CarouselLiveRegion are available from `@pimolu/react`.
- MultiCarousel counts reachable pages: its dots stop at the end of the rail, a swipe keeps the active page synchronized and `slidesPerView` adapts to the cards that actually fit.
- Coverflow, Stack and Arc controls stay above transformed cards on touch screens.
- The Carousels overview compares the seven references and explains the simple versus advanced choice.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="modal" label="New" title="Modal family">

- Modal adds a centered conversation with sm, md, lg and xl sizes, focus containment, Escape and overlay dismissal, scroll lock and focus return.
- ModalHeader, ModalTitle, ModalDescription and ModalFooter structure the panel; ModalClose wraps an existing button without nesting one.
- The optional close button, size axis, `classNames` parts and panel `style` match the documented Dialog contract.
- Dialog remains the lighter name for existing applications; both share the same internals and focusable-trigger helper.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="focus" label="Fixed" title="Keyboard reach for cloned triggers">

- DialogTrigger, DrawerTrigger and DropdownMenuTrigger accept `tabIndex` and add `tabIndex={0}` plus `role="button"` when the child is not natively focusable.
- Popover, Tooltip, HoverCard, ContextMenu and Popconfirm apply the same rule through the shared helper.
- Native controls and explicit roles keep their behaviour; the helper does not overwrite them.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="migration" label="Migration" title="Upgrading from beta.8">

- No public component or hook was removed. New carousel parts, Modal components and trigger behaviour are additive.
- Existing Dialog, Drawer, DropdownMenu, Popover, Tooltip, HoverCard, ContextMenu and Popconfirm markup keeps working.
- Non-focusable trigger children that relied on pointer-only access now receive a tab stop and a button role; remove custom duplicate workarounds.
- `Carousel` keeps its existing API. Choose a carousel family only when its layout is the point.

</ReleaseSection>

<ReleaseSection id="limits" label="Preview" title="Known limits">

- This is a beta: component APIs and visual tokens may still change before 0.1.0 stable.
- The 5–8 person design review and the assistive-technology review have not been executed on hardware. The automated structural and axe checks are not a screen-reader certification.
- MultiCarousel `loop` returns to the first page after the last page; the jump is functional but not seamless.
- The six layout families intentionally omit autoplay. Use `Carousel` or compose your own timer.
- Native, Angular and web registries remain unimplemented.

</ReleaseSection>
